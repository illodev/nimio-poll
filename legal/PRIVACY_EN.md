# Privacy Policy – Nimio Poll

Effective date: January 15, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

- We only store the minimum data required for poll functionality: questions, options, votes, and associated metadata.
- We do not store private messages or sensitive user information.

## 2. Use of Information

- Data is used exclusively to display and manage polls within Slack.
- Data is not used for commercial purposes nor shared with third parties.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

- Poll data is automatically deleted after expiration or manual removal.
- Users may request immediate deletion of their polls at any time.

## 4. Security

- Data is stored in Upstash Redis, encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Reasonable measures are taken to protect information from unauthorized access.

## 5. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy at any time. Changes will be notified by updating this document.
